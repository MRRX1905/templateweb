Rimbun

Rimbun is a display grotesque in nine weights, drawn for long headlines on small screens.

420

One axis holds the whole page.

Type is read at three distances

Rimbun began as a poster face and ended up doing most of its work at 17 pixels. The counters stay open below 24 pixels, the terminals are cut flat so small sizes do not smear, and the narrow proportions let one headline hold two more words per line than a normal width grotesque.
