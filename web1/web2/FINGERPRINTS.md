## Length band

Build 1 lands at 9.9 viewport heights across 5 acts. Build 2 is planned at roughly
7 to 8 viewport heights across 5 chapters with a single pinned act, which keeps it
out of the 6-to-7 acts at 13.6 to 13.8 viewport-height band that the skill lists as
a fingerprint dimension.

## Route note

Build 2 generates no assets: the owner's earlier decision was code-only visuals, so
the image-generation pipeline (`kie.mjs`) was not used and no API key was needed.

## Bans held

- No photographic ground, no scrims, no cards, no `pan` rail, no `scrub`: all four
  are banned by the typographic poster grammar.
- No parallax on the type itself; the planes that move are paper, specks and marks.
- Four device families in use (flow, kinetic characters, reveal wipe, pin), none
  twice in a row.
- No invented statistics, no fabricated figures, no prices.
