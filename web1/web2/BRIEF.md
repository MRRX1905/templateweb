# BRIEF — Tapak Aksara (web2)

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation: the request was "web lain random
aja, pakai skill yang tadi, buat di folder baru, web2". No interview was run, so
every decision below is mine, marked as authored, not transcribed from the owner.

Inherited constraint from the owner's earlier choice on web1: **visuals are code
only**. No generated imagery, no photography, no API keys. That is not a fallback
here, it is the point: a foundry's world is a printed specimen sheet.

## 1. Vibe

Bone paper, cobalt printer's ink, one ink-black chapter in the middle. References:
a foundry specimen book, an architect's drawing sheet (baseline grid, crop marks,
a folio), and a hardware control panel with exactly one physical axis.

## 2. The scroll journey

```
1 Hero      one word at extreme scale, and the axis control under it
2 Bukti     the same sentence set at three sizes, billed as proof
3 Rentang   the ink chapter: scrolling walks the weight axis from 200 to 800
4 Cetak     your own word, set live at three sizes with the axis you left behind
5 Kolofon   the smallest type on the page: licence facts, index, one underlined ask
```

## 3. Energy

Calm at the start (a poster is quiet), a rising middle once the axis starts moving
under the hand, the loudest and darkest frame at Rentang, then deliberately quiet
again at Cetak and almost silent at Kolofon. A page that is loud the whole way is
as flat as one that is quiet the whole way.

## 4. Feeling curve, one line per act

| Act | Emotion | What causes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Curiosity | A six-letter word the size of the window, and a slider that visibly moves it |
| Bukti | Settling in | Lines that arrive character by character, then a paragraph you actually read |
| Rentang | Rising control | The ground cuts to ink and the scroll itself turns hairline into black |
| Cetak | Delight, quieter | Your own word, at your own weight, at three sizes at once |
| Kolofon | Relief, invitation | Smallest type on the page and one plain underlined ask |

No two adjacent acts carry the same feeling.

## 5. The peak

**Rentang**, the ink chapter, with the largest span on the page (4 viewports
against ~1 for each other act). What a visitor should be able to say to a friend:

> "You scroll and the type goes from hairline to black, and then you type your own
> name and it does the same thing."

The act before it, Bukti, is deliberately the quietest thing on the page.

## 6. Tell-someone sentence

It is the site where the scroll is the weight knob.

## 7. One thing this site does that no site they have seen does

The weight axis is a single page-wide value with two drivers. The slider in the
hero sets it, the scroll in Rentang walks it, the value readout moves with both,
and the word you type in Cetak inherits whatever you left it at. Nothing on the
page is weight-static except the paper.

## 8. Aesthetic range

Poster: typographic, high contrast in scale, no cards, no photography, no
decoration that is not either type or a printer's mark.

## 9. One unbroken world, or distinct scenes?

Distinct chapters, with one hard cut of ground in the middle (paper to ink and
back). Not a worldflight: there is no geography to travel through.

## 10. Assets

None owned, none generated. Everything is type, CSS, and one canvas-free SVG
scale. The specimen is set in a Google variable font (Bricolage Grotesque,
wght 200-800) as a placeholder for a real foundry release; the README says so.

## Authored silence

Bukti has one deliberate gap of empty paper between the display line and the
40px specimen line, about 30% of a viewport of nothing. That is authored silence,
not dead scroll, and the verification pass should read it as intentional.
